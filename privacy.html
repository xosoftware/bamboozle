Privacy Policy

BamBoozle — Vegas happy hours, brunch & late night

Effective October 9, 2026 · Applies to the BamBoozle iOS and Android apps and the website at xosoftware.github.io/bamboozle

The short version: BamBoozle has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don’t collect, store or sell your personal data. Everything you do in the app — your location, saved spots and settings — stays on your device.

Location

If you tap “Use my location”, BamBoozle asks your device for your current location (“while using the app” only). It is used only on your device to calculate distances and sort spots nearest-first. Your location is never sent to us or to anyone else, never stored on a server, and never used in the background. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the app still works without it.

Saved spots, settings and alerts

Saved spots, filters, theme and alert preferences are stored locally on your device (app storage). If you turn on saved-spot alerts, reminders are scheduled as local notifications by your device’s operating system. Nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Data we collect

None. BamBoozle does not collect names, emails, contacts, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics or crash reports, and contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Third-party services

  • Map tiles: the map downloads tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org) and, as a fallback, OpenStreetMap. Like any web request, these servers receive your IP address and the map area being viewed; your GPS location is not sent. See the OpenFreeMap and OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.
  • Website hosting: the website and app data updates are served by GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.
  • Links you open: Directions open Apple Maps or Google Maps, and “Open on inKind” or source links open in your browser. Those services’ own privacy policies apply once you leave BamBoozle.

Sharing

If you share a spot, the text is handed to your device’s share sheet and goes only where you choose to send it.

Children

BamBoozle lists bars and alcohol specials and is intended for adults 21 and over. It is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
