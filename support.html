Frequently asked

Are the deals accurate?
Deals, prices, days and hours change often. Listings come from venue websites and third-party guides (last refreshed October 2026) and many are unverified. Always confirm with the venue before you go.

Why does it ask for my location?
Only to sort spots nearest-first and show distances. Your location stays on your device. You can say no and browse the whole list or the map instead.

How do Saved spots and alerts work?
Tap Save on any spot. Use the Saved chip to see only your saved spots. In the app, turn on Saved spot alerts (Filters → Alerts) to get a heads-up 15 minutes before a saved spot’s happy hour, brunch or late-night deal starts. Times are Las Vegas time.

What does the gold inKind badge mean?
The restaurant appeared in inKind’s Las Vegas list when we checked. BamBoozle isn’t affiliated with inKind or any venue.

The map is blank.
Map tiles need an internet connection the first time an area is viewed; the list and venue details work offline.

Is BamBoozle free?
Yes. No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases.

Please drink responsibly. You must be 21+ to purchase alcohol in Nevada.
